# 4.4 Limited Visibility

The player never has complete information. Intelligence arrives filtered through biased sources, delayed by distance, and distorted by the agendas of informants. The player must make decisions under **genuine uncertainty**, which is part of the game's core tension.

- Reports from hosts reflect their personality and worldview, not objective reality.
- Rival societies actively feed the player false information when they can.
- The further the player's influence extends, the less reliable the returning signal.
- Events can unfold in ways the player did not predict and cannot easily understand.
