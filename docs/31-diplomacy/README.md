# 31. Diplomacy — Relationships at Every Level

Diplomatic relationships in Silent Dominion exist at **three distinct levels simultaneously:**

- **State-to-state** relationships are what historians record and diplomats manage.
- **State-to-institution** relationships are what rulers actually depend on day to day.
- **Institution-to-institution** relationships are almost invisible to any actor without deep network access.

The player operates on **all three levels**, and the most powerful plays exploit the gaps between what each level knows about the others.
