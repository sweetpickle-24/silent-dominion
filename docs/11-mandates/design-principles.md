# 11.4 Mandate Design Principles

- No Mandate should require direct action — every phase is achievable through indirect manipulation.
- Mandates should be completable in **multiple ways**, reflecting the player's available network and preferred style.
- **Historical Mandates** (involving real figures) resolve in ways that feel plausible within the [divergence model](../04-world-systems/historical-divergence.md).
- The game **never tells the player how** to complete a Mandate — it only tells them what needs to happen.
- Completing a Mandate leaves a footprint — the less visible your methods, the lower the legacy exposure.
