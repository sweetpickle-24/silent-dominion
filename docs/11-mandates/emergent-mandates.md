# 11.3 Emergent Mandates

Most Mandates are **player-initiated** — you decide what you want to accomplish and activate the corresponding objective.

But some Mandates **trigger automatically** from world events:

- A historical figure rises to dangerous prominence.
- A rival society makes a move that directly threatens your network.
- A window of opportunity opens that will close within a generation if not acted upon.

## How they appear

Emergent Mandates appear as **urgent dispatches on the table** — a sealed letter with a different colour seal, or a report that arrives with a red mark.

The player can **accept, defer, or ignore** them. Ignoring an emergent Mandate means the world resolves it without you — sometimes in ways that cost you significantly.
