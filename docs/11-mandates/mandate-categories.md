# 11.2 Mandate Categories

| **Category** | **Example mandate** | **What it involves** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Removal** | Caesar has become too powerful. He must not become emperor. | Identify allies in the Senate, amplify conspirators' resolve, manage the aftermath of his removal |
| **Elevation** | The Medici are a minor banking family. Make them the power behind Florence. | Protect them from rivals, connect them to the Church, manage their ascendancy over two generations |
| **Ideological** | Plant the seeds of what will become the Reformation 200 years before Luther. | Invest in dissident scholars, protect heretical texts, build the intellectual infrastructure |
| **Survival** | The Inquisition has been handed a description matching you. Disappear completely for 30 years. | Sever every active host, destroy your paper trail, outlast the institutional memory of the hunt |
| **Destabilisation** | The Byzantine Empire's strength threatens your network in the East. Fracture it. | Exploit succession disputes, seed religious schism, manipulate external pressure from multiple directions |
| **Counter-society** | The Architects are close to locking in a permanent centralised order in Northern Europe. Stop them. | Identify their hosts, expose them selectively, fund the decentralising forces they are trying to suppress |
