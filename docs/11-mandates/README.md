# 11. Mandates — Directed Objectives

Mandates are the game's equivalent of strategic focus trees. They are **optional, player-activated objectives** that provide direction, narrative context, and mechanical rewards.

Unlike a focus tree, Mandates are **not a progression system** — they do not unlock content. They are a lens the player chooses to look through for a period of time.

A Mandate frames a goal, breaks it into phases, surfaces relevant actors and events, and tracks progress toward completion. Completing a Mandate does not end the game — it resolves an arc and opens new possibilities. Ignoring a Mandate costs nothing. **The world does not wait.**
