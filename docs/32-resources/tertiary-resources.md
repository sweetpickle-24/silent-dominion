# 32.3 Tertiary Resources — Value and Flavour

Tertiary resources appear as **trade route value modifiers, wealth indicators, and intelligence data.** They do not have their own production chains but affect kingdom wealth levels and trade route attractiveness.

## Examples

- Ivory.
- Precious gems.
- Exotic animals.
- Amber.
- Incense.
- Fine metalwork.

## Mechanical role

Their primary mechanical role is:

- Making certain trade routes extraordinarily valuable.
- Giving the player additional financial leverage in regions where they concentrate.
