# 32.4 Gold and Silver as Separate Currencies

Gold and silver are **separate resources with distinct economic roles.** This is historically accurate and mechanically important.

## Silver — the everyday economy

- Taxes are collected in silver.
- Soldiers are paid in silver.
- Markets operate in silver.

A kingdom short on silver has **immediate domestic problems** — unpaid armies, frozen markets, reduced tax yield. Silver shortage is a faster and more visible crisis than gold shortage.

## Gold — the strategic reserve

- Large transactions use gold.
- War funding uses gold.
- Diplomatic payments use gold.
- Debt settlement uses gold.

A kingdom can have a functioning silver economy but be gold-poor and **unable to fund a prolonged campaign or buy a critical alliance.** Gold shortage is a strategic constraint rather than an immediate crisis.

## Currency debasement

A kingdom that **mixes base metal into its silver coinage** to stretch supply creates short-term relief and long-term inflation.

- The player can detect debasement through financial network intelligence.
- The instability it creates is exploitable.

## Operational routing

The player's financial network operates in **both currencies.**

- Operations that require large transfers use gold.
- Operations embedded in normal commercial activity use silver.
- The routing choice affects discretion and speed.
