# 13.1 What Patterns Look Like

A pattern is a **profile-plus-approach pairing.**

- The **profile** is the set of conditions that characterise a situation — the target's traits, their social context, the relevant institutions around them.
- The **approach** is the sequence of actions that worked.

When the game detects a new situation that matches a profile above a threshold, it offers the automation.

## Pattern examples

| **Pattern type** | **Profile variables** | **What the automation handles** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Wealthy paranoid merchant** | High wealth, high paranoia, low loyalty, mercantile context | Selects the right proxy, chooses fear-based framing, times the approach to a period of business stress |
| **Ambitious minor noble** | High ambition, low current status, susceptible to flattery, weak patron | Identifies the right moment in their career arc, frames elevation offer correctly, manages their patron's reaction |
| **Established religious institution** | High piety weight in region, strong hierarchy, doctrinal rigidity | Routes influence through sympathetic clergy rather than laity, avoids direct doctrinal challenge, works the internal power structure |
| **Paranoid ruler late in reign** | High paranoia, declining health, unstable succession, isolated court | Selects the rumour type most likely to land, times delivery to moments of vulnerability, manages fallout toward desired successor |
| **Reformist intellectual movement** | High literacy in region, accumulated doctrinal grievance, dissident scholar cluster | Identifies the catalytic individual, seeds the specific idea most likely to ignite the movement, manages the institutional response |
