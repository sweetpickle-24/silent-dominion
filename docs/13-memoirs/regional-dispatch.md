# 13.3 Regional Dispatch — Late-Game Automation

By late-game, the player can issue a **regional dispatch** — a high-level instruction that automation executes across an entire area without requiring individual city-level management.

The player sends the instruction to a region; the game routes it to the appropriate cities, identifies the relevant individuals in each, selects the right approach from the library, and executes the operation over the following weeks of game time.

## Examples

- *"Gather economic intelligence across the Levantine coast"* — automation dispatches to every city in the region, routes through established merchant contacts, returns consolidated reports.
- *"Elevate paranoia among northern Frankish nobility"* — automation selects the rumour type that fits the current regional profile, distributes through the appropriate channels, times delivery to maximise effect.
- *"Identify potential hosts in the Italian peninsula"* — automation scans the region for characters matching the player's established cultivation criteria, surfaces the top candidates for manual review.

## Rules

Regional dispatch is **not infallible.**

- It uses the player's intelligence picture, which may be wrong.
- It applies established patterns, which may be stale.
- The player reviews the results when they return — and a series of unexpected failures is a signal that the library needs revalidation in that region.
