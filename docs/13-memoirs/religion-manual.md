# 13.4 Religion and Doctrine — The Manual Requirement

**No religion or ideological movement can be automated until the player has worked through it manually at least once.**

This is a hard rule, not a soft guideline. Every faith, every philosophical school, every political ideology has its own internal logic, power structure, and susceptibility profile. The player must discover these through direct engagement before the game can encode them.

## The manual process for a new religion

- Observing its doctrine and identifying its core appeal.
- Mapping the hierarchy and finding who actually holds power versus who holds title.
- Testing different approach vectors to discover what the institution responds to.
- Identifying the specific grievance points that could be exploited or the belief structures that could be reinforced.

Only when all of these are in the library does automation become available.

## Rules

- A religion the player has never encountered behaves as an **opaque black box** — no automation, no shortcuts.
- A religion the player worked through two centuries ago may have evolved — old automations are flagged as potentially stale if doctrinal or institutional changes are detected.
- **Schisms and reformations force partial relearning** — the pre-schism library applies to the conservative faction but not the reformist one.
- Rival societies exploit gaps in the player's religious library — they operate freely in faiths the player has never engaged.
