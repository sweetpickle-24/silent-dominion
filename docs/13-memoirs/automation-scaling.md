# 13.2 How Automation Scales With Game Stage

The Memoirs system is what allows the game's **decision density to remain consistent** as the player's operational scope expands from a single city to a global network.

Without automation, managing hundreds of simultaneous threads at monthly decision frequency would be impossible. With it, the routine is handled and the player's attention stays on what is genuinely novel.

## Scaling table

| **Game stage** | **Library size** | **What the player manages manually** | **What automation handles** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Early (city)** | Empty — building from scratch | Everything. Every spy dispatch, every letter, every approach decision | Nothing yet |
| **Early-mid (regional)** | A handful of proven patterns | Novel situations, new personality types, unfamiliar institutions | Repeat operations on known profiles in known territory |
| **Mid (kingdom)** | Dozens of patterns across multiple categories | Strategic decisions, new religions, new political structures, crisis response | Routine intelligence gathering, standard host cultivation, known manipulation profiles |
| **Late-mid (multi-kingdom)** | Deep library across most common archetypes | Genuinely novel challenges, new world regions, counter-society operations | Most host management, regional intelligence sweeps, known institution navigation |
| **Late (continental/global)** | Near-complete library for encountered types | High-level strategic direction, truly unprecedented situations, legacy threat management | Entire regions can be instructed at once; automation handles city-level execution |
