# 16. Financial Networks — Gold and Power

The player has **no gold balance.** Centuries of operating in the shadows has taught them that holding wealth directly is an exposure risk and a liability.

What they hold instead is **access** — relationships with banking houses, merchant guilds, and wealthy individuals who can produce funds in specific places when needed. Money is not a number. **It is a geographic and relational resource.**

## 16.1 Financial relationships

Every financial relationship in the player's network has three attributes that determine its value and its risk.

| **Attribute** | **What it means** | **How it changes** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Capacity** | How much funding the relationship can produce in a given period without strain | Grows with relationship depth and the institution's own wealth. Shrinks if overdrawn repeatedly or if the institution faces its own financial stress. |
| **Discretion** | How well the relationship conceals the source and destination of funds | Improves with relationship maturity and with routing infrastructure. Degrades when transactions are large, frequent, or politically sensitive. |
| **Reach** | Which cities and regions the relationship can move money to or from | Expands as the institution grows or as the player connects it to other financial relationships. Geographic gaps require building new relationships in uncovered areas. |

## 16.2 Income and maintenance

Income is **not passive.**

- Financial relationships require servicing — the banker needs to believe the arrangement is mutually beneficial, the merchant house needs to see that cooperation is rewarded.
- A relationship that is not maintained atrophies over months and years.
- One that is pushed too hard — too many large transactions, too many unusual requests — becomes suspicious about who it is actually serving.

### Rules

- Each active financial relationship requires **periodic maintenance** through the coordinator layer — not constant attention but not neglect.
- **Overdrawn relationships** produce funds but generate questions that require explanation through social cover.
- A financial relationship that has become curious about its patron is an **intelligence risk** — the banker who funds your operations knows someone is funding them.
- The wealthiest and most discreet financial infrastructure is **built over generations, not years** — early game the player operates on thin margins and limited reach.

## 16.3 Funding operations

When an operation requires funding, the player issues a **financial instruction** rather than spending from a balance. The system finds the nearest capable financial relationship, calculates how much of its capacity this consumes, assesses the discretion level of the transaction, and executes it through the coordinator layer.

### Transaction routing options

| **Option** | **Detail** |
| --- | --- |
| **Direct payment** | Fastest, cheapest in capacity, highest exposure. The receiving party knows money came from somewhere specific. |
| **Single intermediary** | Moderate cost, moderate discretion. One additional institution between source and destination. |
| **Multi-hop routing** | Expensive in capacity, high discretion. Funds pass through multiple legitimate transactions before reaching destination. Slow. |
| **Embedded in trade** | Most discreet option. Payment is hidden inside a legitimate commercial transaction. Requires a merchant host with the right connections. Takes the most time. |

## 16.4 Financial intelligence exposure

Every significant financial transaction is a **piece of intelligence about the player's operations**, held by the institution that executed it.

> A banking house that has processed a hundred transactions over twenty years holds a mosaic of information about where the player's money flows. They do not know who the player is but they know the pattern. A rival society with access to the right banker has a map of the player's operational geography.

### Rules

- Long-standing financial relationships are **both the most valuable and the most dangerous** — they know the most.
- Periodically **rotating financial relationships** reduces accumulated intelligence exposure but loses relationship depth and capacity.
- A financial relationship that is **turned by a rival society** becomes a perfect intelligence tap — every future transaction is visible to the enemy.
- **Acquiring a financial relationship used by a rival society** gives the same advantage in reverse.
