# 9. The Autonomous World

The world of Silent Dominion does not wait for the player. Every entity in the simulation — every kingdom, ruler, city, trade route, army, and religious institution — has internal state that ticks forward **every game-day regardless of player involvement.** Droughts happen. Kings die. Wars start and end. Philosophies spread and fracture.

The player is **one factor among thousands**, not the engine that makes things move.

This is the game's most important atmospheric commitment. When the player extends a spy into a region they have never touched, they should discover that three kingdoms have risen and fallen there while they were not watching. The world generates its own history in the corners the player ignores.
