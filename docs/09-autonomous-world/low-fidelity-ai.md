# 9.6 Low-Fidelity Ruler AI

In regions where the player has no presence, ruler AI runs in **simplified form.** Major decisions are still made by the same logic but at lower granularity — the actor's traits and the strategic situation produce decisions without simulating named advisors or detailed event chains.

The actor still makes wars, marriages, alliances, and collapses, but the **texture** of those decisions is abstracted.

## Rules

- When the player extends intelligence into a low-fidelity region, the **recent history of decisions** there becomes available — the player can see what was decided even though they were not present.
- Low-fidelity actors **can still be influenced by other secret societies** operating there — the rivals' work is invisible to the player but real in the simulation.
- The transition from low-fidelity to high-fidelity simulation happens automatically as the player's coverage of a region grows.
