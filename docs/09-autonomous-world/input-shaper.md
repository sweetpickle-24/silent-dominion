# 9.5 The Player as Input Shaper

This is where the player's influence model meets autonomous AI. **The player does not make rulers decide — they shape the inputs the rulers are responding to.**

- Cultivating the militarist general means that advisor has more weight in the next decision.
- Planting paranoia about a neighbour's intentions means the king is now interpreting ambiguous events as threats.
- Removing or discrediting the cautious finance minister means one fewer voice arguing against aggressive action.

## Rival societies do the same thing, in parallel

Other secret societies are doing exactly this in parallel, often pushing in opposite directions.

> A ruler whose decisions seem inconsistent over time may be a battleground between competing shadow influences — the player is amplifying the militarist advisor while the Architects are quietly elevating the chancellor. The actor's erratic behaviour is the visible signature of an invisible struggle.

Reading those inconsistencies is one of the ways the player detects [rival activity](../08-map-and-provinces/rival-fingerprints.md).
