# 36. Core Design Principles

These are the north-star principles that every other system in the game defers to. When a design decision is ambiguous, the resolution is always the principle that wins.

| **Principle** | **What it means in practice** |
| --- | --- |
| **Indirection always** | The player never does anything directly. Every action is mediated by a person or system. **There are no direct buttons.** |
| **Patience as mastery** | The best players are the ones who do least. Rushing creates exposure. **Restraint is a skill.** |
| **Consequences are real** | Actions have downstream effects that cannot be cancelled or undone. **The world remembers.** |
| **Uncertainty is the game** | The player is never fully informed. Acting under incomplete information is the core challenge, not solving a perfect puzzle. |
| **No heroes, no villains** | The player is not good or evil. They are **strategic.** The game should never frame their actions in moral terms. |
| **History as raw material** | Historical events are texture, not constraints. The game respects and then breaks from history when the player's actions warrant it. |
| **Rivals as mirrors** | Other secret societies use identical tools. The most dangerous opponents are the ones who understand how you think. |

---

> *The measure of success in Silent Dominion is not what you built. It is how long you lasted, and how little of it could ever be traced back to you.*
