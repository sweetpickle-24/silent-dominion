# The Immortal — Core Nature

## Why the shadows

Every era has its explanation for things it cannot understand.

- **Ancient world** — you are a demigod or a demon depending on who is doing the explaining.
- **Medieval period** — you are a witch, a heretic, a servant of the devil.
- **Early modern period** — you are an occultist, a conspirator, a danger to the natural order.

In every age, the conclusion is the same: burn it.

## The core survival logic

- You are **killable.** Any soldier with a sword can end you if they can find you.
- You are **recognisable.** Stay in one place too long and people notice you do not age.
- You are **memorable.** Appear publicly in one era and chroniclers write you down. Appear again 100 years later and scholars start connecting the dots.

The shadows are not a stylistic choice. They are the only reason you are still alive.

## No avatar, no portrait

- The player has no public name, no permanent face, no fixed identity.
- Everything the player does is mediated through other actors (see [§3 Core Mechanics](../03-core-mechanics/README.md)) or through [cover identities](./cover-identities.md).
- The player interface (the table — [§7](../07-interface/README.md)) never presents the player's own likeness.
