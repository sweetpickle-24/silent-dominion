# Memory as Advantage

The player's most powerful resource is **memory.** While every other actor in the world lives and dies within a generation, the player accumulates centuries of pattern recognition.

- They have seen this type of ruler before.
- They know how this type of empire ends.
- They understand what a religious movement looks like in its early stages before anyone else notices it.

## Mechanical expression

- Player memory persists across all in-game time.
- Pattern recognition unlocks as the player observes recurring cycles (see [Cycle Engine](../04-world-systems/cycle-engine.md)).
- Memory of betrayal, alliance, and consequence shapes future options.
- Other immortals (see [§5](../05-societies/README.md)) share this advantage — making them true peers.

## In-game surfacing

- The [Memoirs system](../13-memoirs/README.md) encodes this accumulated expertise into reusable patterns.
- The [Shadow Figure legend layer](../10-shadow-figure/README.md) — what the world remembers of you — is the mirror image of what you remember of the world.
