# 34.4 The Three Intelligence Channels

Every event, including the results of the player's own operations, arrives through one of three channels. The channels carry the same events but at different speeds with different reliability and different filtering.

| **Channel** | **Speed and reliability** | **What it covers and what it misses** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Operative network** | Fastest for events within coverage; reliability depends on operative quality and compromise status | Covers anything an operative can observe or be told. Can be wrong, delayed, or actively falsified. The player's most detailed source and their most vulnerable one. |
| **Neutral channels** | Slower than operative network; not controlled by any party | Merchants talking, travellers reporting, market price movements, rumour circulation. Cannot be falsified by any individual actor. Used to calibrate operative reports. Misses secret or institutional events entirely. |
| **Official announcements** | Slowest; most authoritative for what they cover | Events that a party chose to publicise. Accurate for what they state; deliberately omits what they wish to suppress. A royal death announcement is reliable; its succession implications are not. |
