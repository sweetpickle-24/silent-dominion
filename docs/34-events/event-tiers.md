# 34.1 Event Tiers by Scale

| **Tier** | **Scope** | **Examples** | **Who hears it** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Worldwide** | Global — everyone eventually hears | Solar eclipse, fall of a major empire, pandemic plague, declaration of a new world religion | All actors, with time delay proportional to distance and infrastructure quality |
| **State** | Kingdom and neighbours first, then spreading outward | Royal death, declaration of war, famine, major battle outcome, coronation | Neighbouring kingdoms quickly via diplomatic channels; distant kingdoms slowly via merchant and traveller networks |
| **Factional** | Spreads through specific institutional networks, not geographically | Papal pronouncement, guild bankruptcy, scholarly publication, secret society internal event | Members and contacts of the relevant institution, regardless of geography; outsiders much later or never |
| **Local** | Contained within a city or province unless it grows | City fire, minor rebellion, official arrested, supply route disrupted, local bribe | Only those physically present or with active operative coverage; does not propagate unless it escalates |
