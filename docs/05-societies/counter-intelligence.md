# Society Detection and Counter-Intelligence

Other societies operate the same exposure mechanics as the player. If you can detect a rival's footprint in the world — patterns of influence that don't serve any visible actor's interest — you can identify their current hosts and begin counter-operations. **They can do the same to you.**

## Counter-intelligence mechanics

- Counter-intelligence is its own action category, costing organisation bandwidth and exposure.
- **Exposing a rival society's host to their patron** is a high-impact aggressive move.
- Societies can "burn" each other's networks the same way a paranoid king would.
- The most dangerous situation: **two societies competing for the same host.**
- Identifying that a society's immortal is alive vs. dead vs. contested is among the most valuable strategic intelligence in the game.
