# 5. Other Immortals and Their Societies

The player is not alone. Across the recorded history of the world there are perhaps **five to nine other immortals** — human beings who, like the player, simply do not die. They did not choose this. They have figured it out independently, each in their own time, and each has learned the same fundamental lesson the player has: survival requires shadows.

They are the player's **only true peers.** There are perhaps three to five of them alive at any given moment in the game.

Each immortal has, over centuries, built or inherited an organisation around themselves. These organisations are what the world calls **secret societies.** They are not ageless conspiracies of immortals — they are mortal institutions built by immortal founders, sustained by mortal members across generations, sometimes outliving the immortal who created them and sometimes dying when their immortal founder is finally killed.
