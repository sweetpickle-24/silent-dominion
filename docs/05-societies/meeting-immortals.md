# Meeting Another Immortal

In rare cases the player can directly encounter another immortal. This is not a routine event — it requires deep investigation, patient triangulation, and usually the immortal's own decision to allow contact. When it happens, it is one of the most consequential moments in any playthrough.

Other immortals are the player's **only true peers.**

- They remember the world as it was three centuries ago.
- They understand survival as the player understands it.
- They have made their own choices about which side of which conflicts to be on, what kind of organisation to build, what their long-term agenda is.

Negotiation between immortals is possible. Cooperation is possible. So is the cold understanding that one of you may eventually have to kill the other.

## Risks and dynamics

- **Direct meetings carry enormous personal exposure risk** — if you are physically together, both immortals can be killed by the same accident.
- **Communication through intermediaries** is more common than direct meetings.
- Immortal-to-immortal agreements are not enforceable but tend to be honoured because both parties understand the alternative.
- **Killing another immortal is possible but extremely difficult** — they have all of your survival skills plus their own organisation's protection.
- An immortal who knows your real identity and survives your attempt to kill them becomes **the most dangerous threat in the game.**
