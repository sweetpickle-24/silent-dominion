# Immortals and Their Societies — States and Vulnerabilities

Each named secret society has a different relationship to its immortal founder, and these differences shape how it operates and how it can be attacked.

| **Society state** | **What this means** | **Vulnerability** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Active immortal** | The founding immortal is still alive and personally directing the society | Killing the immortal cripples the society. Their organisation may persist but loses cohesion and direction. |
| **Posthumous society** | The founding immortal has died; the organisation runs on accumulated culture and mortal succession | Cannot be decapitated by killing an immortal. Must be attacked structurally through corruption, succession disruption, or ideological subversion. |
| **Contested succession** | The founding immortal recently died; mortal claimants are competing for leadership | Maximum vulnerability. Internal conflict opens windows for outside manipulation. The society may fragment or change direction radically. |
| **Reformed society** | The original immortal is gone but a new immortal has taken control of the organisation | Behaves like an active immortal society again, but the organisation's culture may not match the new immortal's vision — internal friction is exploitable. |

## Strategic implication

- Identifying whether a rival society's immortal is **alive / dead / contested** is among the most valuable intelligence in the game.
- This information is always hidden at the start. The player learns it through [investigation](./counter-intelligence.md).
