# 22.5 Travel Speed by Era

Travel time across the map reflects the **actual constraints of each historical period.** An overland journey from Rome to Alexandria takes very different amounts of game-time in 200 BCE versus 1600 CE.

The era-appropriate travel speed is part of what makes historical texture feel real rather than abstracted.

| **Era** | **Overland speed** | **Sea speed (favourable)** | **Effect on operations** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ancient world** | ~30–40 km/day on Roman roads; less elsewhere | ~100–150 km/day | Major moves take weeks to months. Intelligence lag is measured in months for distant regions. |
| **Medieval** | ~25–35 km/day; road quality degraded | ~80–120 km/day | Similar to ancient. Feudal borders create political friction that slows movement beyond physical distance. |
| **Early modern** | ~40–60 km/day; improved roads | ~120–200 km/day | Moves are faster. Postal networks begin to reduce intelligence lag even when the player is in transit. |
| **Modern (1700s+)** | ~100–400 km/day with rail | ~300–500 km/day with steam | Moves become almost instantaneous relative to earlier eras. The transition window dominates over travel time. |
