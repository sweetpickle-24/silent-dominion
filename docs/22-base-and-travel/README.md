# 22. Base of Operations — Moving and Travel

The player's base of operations is the **physical location their table occupies** — the city they are currently working from.

It is **not a permanent home.** An immortal operating across centuries follows power. When the centre of gravity shifts — when a new empire rises, when a new trade hub emerges, when a developing situation demands physical proximity — the player moves.

Moving is a **strategic decision driven by opportunity**, not a safety cycle driven by exposure management.

Because the player never presents themselves publicly, travel carries minimal inherent exposure. They move as a merchant, a scholar, a pilgrim — an unremarkable figure on the road. Nobody is searching for them specifically. The danger of travel is not visibility. It is the **vulnerability window**: stale intelligence along the route, no established network at waypoints, and the inability to respond quickly to events while in transit.
