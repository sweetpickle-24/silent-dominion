# 22.4 The Transition Window

After arriving, a **transition window of days to weeks** applies before the new base is fully operational.

During this window:

- The player's intelligence picture of the new location is freshly assembled rather than current — the local coordinator has prepared a summary but it reflects their knowledge, not the player's direct engagement.
- Some source reports are delayed as correspondence routes finish rerouting.
- The [Memoirs automation system](../13-memoirs/README.md) runs at **reduced reliability** in the new location until the player has re-established direct familiarity with local conditions.

## Rules

- Intelligence from the **previous base location continues arriving normally** — the organisation there does not stop running.
- The new location's **fog of knowledge is higher** than it will be after a month of settled operation.
- The **first weeks in a new base** are among the richest for discovery — the player is actively learning a new political environment.
- Rival societies who track the player's movement pattern may attempt an operation during the transition window, knowing the player's local responsiveness is reduced.
