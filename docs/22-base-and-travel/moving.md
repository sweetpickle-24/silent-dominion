# 22.1 The Move as a Strategic Act

The player moves when the world makes it worthwhile — **not on a timer, not to manage a legend meter.**

A move might happen once in fifty years or three times in a decade depending on what history is doing.

> The question is always: **where does the next century of meaningful action happen, and am I positioned to be part of it from the beginning?**

## Move triggers

- Move toward a **rising power** to establish deep roots before everyone else recognises its importance.
- Move toward a **developing crisis** that the current base cannot cover effectively through remote coordination.
- Move **away from a collapsing structure** before being dragged down with it — the [pivot decision](../06-progression-and-time/pivot-mechanic.md) made physical.
- Move to a region where a **rival society is operating** to bring the player's personal attention to the counter-operation.
- Move to **follow a key family or lieutenant** whose relocation changes the organisation's centre of gravity.
