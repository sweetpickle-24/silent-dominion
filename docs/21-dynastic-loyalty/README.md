# 21. Dynastic Loyalty — Family Trees and Generational Service

Influence through individuals is fragile. A lieutenant dies, a coordinator is burned, an operative turns — the relationship ends and the work of replacement begins.

**Dynastic loyalty is the answer to this fragility.** When the player cultivates not just a person but a family — protecting them through crises, advancing their interests, shaping their culture over generations — the family becomes a persistent organisational node that replaces its own members without requiring the player to start over.

The Medici are the archetype. The player does not simply have a banker named Cosimo. They have a family that has understood for two centuries that there is a silent patron whose interests must always be served. The grandfather built the bank under the player's guidance. The father expanded it. The current head grew up hearing the stories. The next generation is already being trained. When Cosimo dies, the institution does not miss a step because **the institution is the family, not the man.**
