# 21.2 Family Trees — The Succession Model

Every family in the player's network has a **family tree** that the game tracks across generations. The tree is not cosmetic — it is the succession plan.

> When the current head of a family dies, the next generation is already in the tree with their own trait scores, their own stat profiles, and their own relationship to the player.

## Weighted inheritance

Children of loyal families carry weighted traits shaped by their upbringing.

- A child raised in a banking family that has served the player for three generations is weighted toward financial competence, discretion, and loyalty.
- A child raised in a family that felt abandoned during a crisis carries a different weight — resentment, ambition, a desire to prove independence.

**The player's treatment of families over time writes itself into the next generation's character before the player has ever met them.**

## Family history → next generation

| **Family history** | **Trait weight shift in next generation** | **Practical implication** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Player protected family through a major crisis** | Loyalty +, Trust in player +, Willingness to take risk + | Successor takes the role with deep personal commitment, not just inherited obligation |
| **Player advanced family's status and wealth consistently** | Ambition +, Competence +, Expectation of continued advancement + | Successor is more capable but may become demanding — expects the relationship to keep paying |
| **Player ignored family during a crisis they needed help** | Resentment +, Loyalty -, Independence + | Successor serves but is looking for alternatives. A rival offer at the right moment will land. |
| **Player over-relied on family without reciprocating** | Fatigue +, Obligation felt as burden +, Quiet resistance + | Successor complies but performs below capability. The relationship is transactional rather than loyal. |
| **Player invested in children's education and development** | Intellect +, Domain expertise +, Personal gratitude to player + | Successor has genuinely expanded capabilities and a direct personal bond with the player beyond inherited obligation |
