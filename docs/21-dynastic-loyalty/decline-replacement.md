# 21.6 Family Decline and Replacement

Not every family serves forever. Families decline through:

- Exhaustion.
- Ideological drift.
- Over-elevation into public prominence.
- The simple failure to produce a capable next generation.

The player must monitor family trajectories and sometimes make the painful decision to **let a loyal dynasty fade** rather than prop up a degraded successor who will damage the operations they are supposed to run.

## Signals and scenarios

- **Family decline signals** — consecutive generations with lower stat ceilings, increasing resentment scores, requests the player cannot or will not meet, public prominence that creates exposure risk.
- **Managed decline** — gradually reduce the family's operational role while maintaining the relationship. They step back; a new family steps up. Done well, the old family becomes a silent asset rather than a dead weight.
- **Catastrophic decline** — a family that has been over-relied on without maintenance fails suddenly — corruption, defection, public scandal, or enemy targeting. The player loses the entity layer they were running simultaneously with the family.
- **Replacement** — identifying and beginning to cultivate the next family before the current one degrades is the mark of **experienced play.** The transition between loyal dynasties is the most vulnerable moment in the organisation's long-term stability.
