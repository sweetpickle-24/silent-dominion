# 21.1 The Family as an Organisational Unit

A powerful family is a **persistent organisational node that happens to be operated by changing individuals.**

It carries:

- Institutional memory.
- Inherited loyalty.
- Domain expertise.
- A cultural identity shaped by the player's long relationship with it.

These properties **compound across generations** in a way that recruited individuals never can.

## What a family accumulates across generations

| **Dimension** | **Detail** |
| --- | --- |
| **Institutional loyalty** | Not a trait score but a cultural inheritance. Children grow up knowing the silent patron exists and that serving them is what this family does. |
| **Domain expertise** | Each generation inherits the knowledge base of the previous one and builds on it. A banking family in its fourth generation is far more capable than a first-generation recruit. |
| **Inherited relationships** | The family's contacts, social position, and reputation in their domain persist through individual deaths. |
| **Operational culture** | Familiarity with shadow arrangements, unusual requests, and the player's methods. Less shock, less risk of accidental exposure. |
| **Accumulated intelligence** | Family archives hold decades of correspondence, records, and institutional memory that no individual could carry alone. |
