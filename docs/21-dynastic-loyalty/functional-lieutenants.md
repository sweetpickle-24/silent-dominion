# 21.5 The Functional Lieutenant Structure

The organisation's lieutenant layer should be **functionally specialised, not only geographically.**

Each functional lieutenant manages a domain across multiple regions, with coordinators running operations within their domain. This structure is more resilient than geographic organisation alone because functional expertise accumulates at the lieutenant level and does not need to be rebuilt when a region changes hands.

## Lieutenant types

| **Lieutenant type** | **What they manage** | **Ideal family background** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Financial lieutenant** | All owned entities — banks, trading companies, guild holdings. Sets commercial strategy, manages entity coordinators, routes financial operations. | Banking or merchant dynasty with multi-generational financial expertise and deep trade network relationships |
| **Intelligence lieutenant** | Spy networks, information flows, counter-intelligence. Filters and routes intelligence upward, directs targeted operations downward. | Family with deep administrative or diplomatic history; comfort with information as a commodity |
| **Ideological lieutenant** | Scholarly academies, religious houses, philosophical networks. Shapes cultural infrastructure and manages the idea pipeline. | Scholarly or clerical family with generational intellectual standing and institutional church or academy connections |
| **Security lieutenant** | Counter-intelligence, protection operations, handling threats to the organisation's members and entities. | Military or administrative family with loyalty as their dominant inherited trait; history of service under pressure |
| **Political lieutenant** | Host cultivation among rulers and courts, succession management, factional influence. | Noble or diplomatic family with natural access to courts and a multi-generational history of political positioning |

## Family-lieutenant synergy

A lieutenant drawn from or deeply connected to a powerful loyal family in their domain has **natural authority** that a recruited outsider would spend years trying to establish.

> The financial lieutenant who has been a Medici cousin for thirty years does not need to introduce themselves to the banking network — they **are** the banking network.

**This is why investing in families is investing in lieutenants two generations before you need them.**
