# 21.3 Family Needs and the Loyalty Maintenance Loop

Families have needs that must be addressed to maintain loyalty. **Not just payment** — status, protection, advancement, and help in crises.

The moments that define the relationship are the **moments of need**:

- A family member imprisoned.
- A business rival trying to destroy them.
- A blackmail threat that they cannot handle alone.

**What the player does in those moments is the relationship.**

> A player who consistently shows up — who gets the son released, who neutralises the blackmailer, who ensures the rival fails at a critical moment — builds the kind of loyalty that cannot be bought or turned by a rival society's approach. These families do not serve because they are paid. They serve because they know from lived experience that the player protects what they care about. **That is a completely different kind of reliability.**

## Family needs catalogue

| **Family need type** | **What triggers it** | **Player response and effect** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Member in legal or political danger** | Family member arrested, accused, or targeted by a hostile ruler | Player intervenes through political host or coordinator. Costs financial capacity and exposure. Loyalty +++ if successful. Permanent grievance if ignored. |
| **Blackmail or external threat** | Family member approached by a rival society or hostile faction | Player neutralises the threat. Reveals the rival network attempting the approach. Deep loyalty if handled well. |
| **Business or financial crisis** | Entity under the family's management facing collapse | Player routes emergency financial support or political cover. Entity survives. Family's dependence on player deepens. |
| **Succession dispute within the family** | Multiple heirs with competing claims or incompatible traits | Player mediates, backs the right heir, shapes the outcome. Chosen heir has strong personal loyalty. Bypassed heir may become a problem. |
| **Request for status advancement** | Family has served well and wants public recognition or political elevation | Player advances them through a political host. Strengthens the entity's institutional position. Slightly increases public profile of the family. |
| **Ideological drift in next generation** | A family member develops beliefs inconsistent with the player's agenda | Player can invest in the member's development to redirect them, arrange a mentor, or accept the drift and manage around it. |
