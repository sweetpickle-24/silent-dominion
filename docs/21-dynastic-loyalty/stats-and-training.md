# 21.4 Stats, Training and Underdog Development

Every member of the organisation has a stat profile that starts at their natural ability level and can be improved through experience and deliberate investment.

**Training is domain-specific, family-influenced, and bounded by each individual's natural ceiling** — which training cannot exceed but which varies widely between individuals.

## Stat catalogue

| **Stat** | **What it governs** | **How it is developed** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Operational competence** | Quality of execution in their role — fewer misfires, faster results | Direct experience over time; formal training by a more capable superior; mentorship within the family |
| **Discretion** | How cleanly they operate without leaving traces | Experience with high-stakes operations; deliberate training in counter-surveillance; exposure to the player's operational culture |
| **Loyalty** | Resistance to corruption, rival approaches, and personal temptation | Positive treatment over time; rescue during crises; personal relationship depth; family culture |
| **Corruption resistance** | How hard they are to turn or bribe by rivals or circumstances | Loyalty + financial stability + personal ideological commitment. Cannot be directly trained — must be cultivated through conditions |
| **Domain expertise** | Depth of knowledge in their specific function | Years in role; formal education investment; family inheritance; exposure to high-quality mentors |
| **Leadership** | Ability to manage the layer below them effectively | Command experience; gradual elevation through expanding responsibility; natural ceiling varies widely |

## Natural ceilings

The natural ceiling matters because it creates a **realistic distinction between functional and exceptional.**

- Most coordinators can be trained to competence.
- A truly exceptional lieutenant — someone with high-ceiling traits across multiple stats, deep domain expertise, and inherited loyalty — is **rare.**
- Finding one is a significant event.
- **Developing one from a promising child in a loyal family over twenty years is one of the most satisfying long-term investments in the game.**
