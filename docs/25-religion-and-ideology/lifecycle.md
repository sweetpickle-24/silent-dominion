# 25.2 Religion Lifecycle

Religions are **not static.** They emerge, spread, fracture, reform, and sometimes die. The lifecycle has **five phases** and the player can intervene in any of them.

## The five phases

### Emergence

A spiritual vacuum exists, a charismatic figure appears, an idea begins spreading through the population.

> **Player options:** seed the initial idea, cultivate the founding figure, or suppress the emergence before it gains mass.

### Consolidation

The religion establishes its doctrine, hierarchy, and geographic base. Institutional strength builds. **This is the period when the structure is most shapeable** — the player can influence which doctrines consolidate and who controls the hierarchy.

### Dominance

The religion is the established cultural and often political authority in its region. **Hardest to manipulate directly.** Best attacked through reform potential and ideological undercurrents.

### Fracture

Accumulated reform potential, external pressure, or deliberate destabilisation produces schism. The religion splits into factions.

> **Player options:** direct which faction prevails, redirect the splinter toward useful doctrines, or ensure both factions are too weak to oppose the player's agenda.

### Decline

The religion loses popular depth and institutional strength. Often replaced by a new spiritual movement filling the resulting vacuum.

> **Player options:** accelerate decline or harvest the dying institution for its remaining influence.
