# 7.5 Time Controls

Time flows in three units: **days, months, and years.** The player can switch between speeds at any time or pause entirely. The experience mirrors HoI4's time system but without hours — the smallest meaningful unit in Silent Dominion is a day, since most consequential actions unfold over days at minimum.

| **Speed** | **Real time** | **Best used for** | **Game time per minute** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Paused** | Stopped | Reading reports, composing letters, planning | — |
| **Day speed (x1)** | 1 day / second | Active crises, battles resolving, key decisions | ~1 month |
| **Month speed (x2)** | 1 month / second | Normal play — watching plans develop | ~1 year |
| **Year speed (x3)** | 1 year / second | Waiting out exposure decay, long-horizon plans | ~1 decade |
| **Decade speed (x4)** | 1 decade / second | Bridging quiet centuries, waiting for cycles to turn | ~1 century |

## Auto-pause and auto-slow

The game **auto-pauses and drops to day speed** when events require player attention:

- A letter arrives that needs a reply.
- A host is about to make a consequential decision.
- A rival society makes a detectable move.
- A crisis escalates beyond a threshold.

Rich short events — a specific battle, an assassination attempt, a succession struggle — run at day speed for their full duration, then return to the player's chosen speed automatically.
