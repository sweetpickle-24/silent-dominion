# 7.3 + 8.4 The Map and Zoom Levels

## The map

The map is the largest object on the table and the player's primary view of the world. It is always drawn in the cartographic style of the current era.

- **Ancient era** — hand-drawn on papyrus, Mediterranean-centric, landmasses approximate, seas labelled with myth.
- **Medieval era** — drawn on vellum, Jerusalem at centre, compass roses, illustrations of sea monsters.
- **Early modern era** — printed on paper, coastal detail sharp, interiors still speculative, grid lines emerging.
- **Modern era** — engraved on copper-plate paper, national borders clear, full global projection.

The map does **not** show unit positions or military movements in real time. It shows political control, with regions shading to reflect the player's knowledge. Areas the player has no intelligence on are shown as they would appear on a map of the era — which may be completely wrong.

## Zoom levels

The map operates at four zoom levels. Each level reveals different information and enables different interactions. Zooming is **seamless** — the map transitions continuously rather than snapping between discrete views.

| **Zoom level** | **What is visible** | **What can be interacted with** | **Scale** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **World** | Kingdom borders, capitals, major geographic features, era-appropriate cartography | Select kingdoms, view political status, scan for macro events | Full world to continental |
| **Regional** | Province boundaries, terrain types, all settlements, road and river networks, active trade routes | Select provinces, view regional political dynamics, assign coverage, issue regional dispatches | Continental to multi-kingdom |
| **Province** | Individual buildings in settlements, resource nodes, fortifications, troop positions if known, infrastructure under construction | Select individuals, access dossiers, issue specific instructions, view infrastructure slots | Single kingdom to city scale |
| **City district** | City districts with their functions, key buildings, named individuals present, active operations visible to the player | Direct dossier access, local network management, building influence operations | Single city |

See also [§8.5 City Districts](../08-map-and-provinces/city-districts.md) for the innermost zoom detail.
