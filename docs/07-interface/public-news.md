# 7.7 Public News as Calibration

Not all information arrives through the player's network. Major world events generate **public knowledge** that reaches everyone regardless of intelligence access — a king dying in battle, a city sacked and burned, a famine that triggers mass migration, a new religion declared.

These events arrive as public dispatches on the table: brief, undeniable, and available to all.

## How the player uses public news

The player's job is to read public news not just for the event itself but for what it reveals about their intelligence picture.

> A public dispatch announces that the northern trade route through Carthage was destroyed in a raid three years ago. The player's trade layer still shows it active and profitable. Someone in their network has been feeding them false data for three years.

Finding **who** — and whether it was incompetence or deliberate betrayal — is the investigation that follows.

## Rules of public news

- Public news events fire **regardless of player network coverage.**
- They are **always accurate** — they represent information so widely known it cannot be falsified.
- Comparing public news against the intelligence picture is the **primary method** of detecting compromised sources.
- Rival societies **cannot suppress public news events** but can delay the player receiving them if their courier network is disrupted.
