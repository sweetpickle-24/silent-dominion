# 6.3 The Pivot Mechanic — Switching Empires

One of the player's most important strategic decisions is **when to abandon a failing power structure and attach to its successor.**

- Staying too long in a declining empire means being dragged down with it.
- Moving too early means losing the influence accumulated in the current structure.

## The pivot decision

| **Axis** | **Consideration** |
| --- | --- |
| **Signal to pivot** | The empire's trajectory is irreversible and the next power centre is identifiable |
| **Cost of pivoting** | All host relationships in the old structure are severed or reduced |
| **Benefit of pivoting** | Early attachment to a rising power gives maximum long-term leverage |
| **Risk** | The empire you abandoned may recover — and remember your footprint |

## Heuristics

- Watch the [cycle engine](../04-world-systems/cycle-engine.md) signals. A republic sliding toward imperial concentration is often the pivot moment.
- Track [treasury conditions](../32-resources/taxation-and-treasury.md) of neighbouring powers — the rising treasury usually precedes the rising empire by decades.
- Never pivot without first establishing a [base-of-operations move](../22-base-and-travel/README.md) toward the new centre. The pivot is both relational and physical.
