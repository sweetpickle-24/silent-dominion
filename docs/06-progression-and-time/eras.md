# 6.2 Era Progression

The game begins in **Ancient Greece (approximately 500 BCE)** and moves forward through time as the player acts and history unfolds. Eras are **not strictly bounded** — they shift when the underlying dynamics of the world change, not on a calendar.

| **Era** | **Approx. period** | **Key dynamics for the player** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ancient world** | 500 BCE – 200 CE | City-states, philosophical movements, early empire formation. Ideas spread slowly. Religion is local and polytheistic. Trade networks are young. |
| **Classical collapse** | 200 – 600 CE | Imperial overextension, mass migration, spiritual vacuum. High chaos — maximum opportunity and maximum danger. Old networks are worthless. |
| **Medieval consolidation** | 600 – 1300 CE | Feudal structures, dominant faiths, slow information travel. Long-horizon play rewards patience. Merchants begin their ascendancy. |
| **Early modern fracture** | 1300 – 1700 CE | Printing press, reformation, global trade. Ideas now travel at speed. Nation-states emerge. Old feudal hosts lose value. |
| **Modern era** | 1700 CE onward | Mass politics, nationalism, public opinion. Shadow manipulation becomes harder as transparency increases. New tools required. |
