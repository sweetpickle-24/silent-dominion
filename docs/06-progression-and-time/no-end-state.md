# 6.1 No End State — The Sandbox Model

Silent Dominion has **no victory condition and no ending.** Like HoI4 or Dwarf Fortress, the game generates its own goals and meaning. Empires rise and fall. The player defines their own ambition in each era, abandons it when circumstances change, and defines a new one.

## Player-defined objectives

The game is structured around **self-imposed goals** that give direction to each era of play without ever ending the game when achieved:

- **Stability objective** — maintain a specific power structure or culture for 200+ years.
- **Domination objective** — ensure a single ideology becomes the dominant worldview in a region.
- **Collapse objective** — bring down a specific empire or dynasty.
- **Bloodline objective** — elevate a specific minor family to continental dominance across generations.
- **Survival objective** — outlast a period of maximum danger without being exposed.

## The system equivalent

These player-defined objectives are formalised as [Mandates](../11-mandates/README.md) — directed objectives the player can activate for narrative context, phase tracking, and mechanical rewards.

Nothing is ever required. Ignoring a Mandate costs nothing. The world does not wait.
