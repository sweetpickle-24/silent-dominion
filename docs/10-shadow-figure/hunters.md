# 10.5 Characters Who Seek You — Hunters

Some generated characters, given the right personality weights and circumstances, become **hunters** — individuals who have become convinced the shadow figure is real and have made its exposure their life's work.

These are among the most dangerous characters in the game because they are **not acting on orders** from a ruler or a rival society. They are acting on personal conviction, which makes them resistant to most standard manipulation tools.

## Hunter emergence

- Hunters are most likely to emerge from **high-intellect, high-paranoia, low-loyalty** character pools — exactly the populations the player tends to create through destabilisation.
- A hunter with **institutional backing** (a papal mandate, a ruler's commission) is a crisis-level threat.
- Hunters can be discredited, redirected toward a false target, or removed — but removal of a publicly known figure spikes exposure.
- **The most dangerous outcome:** a hunter who dies but whose notes survive and are found by a successor a generation later.
