# 10.4 The Chronicle Threat — Written Records

Characters who get close to the truth **write it down.** Over centuries, a scattered archive of dangerous documents accumulates across the world — a philosopher's private treatise, a papal investigator's report, a Medici family note passed through generations, a scholar's obsessive analysis of anomalous events spanning three hundred years.

Each document is a fragment. Together they form a picture that could expose everything.

## How the threat loop works

Every few generations, one of these documents surfaces — rediscovered in a monastery library, cited in a new scholarly work, presented to a ruler as evidence of conspiracy. The player receives intelligence that it exists. From that point it is a race: find it and suppress it before the wrong person reads it and starts connecting the modern dots to the historical record.

## The chronicle threat loop

1. A character who gets close to identifying the player writes a document before they can be neutralised.
2. The document enters the world — placed in an archive, copied, sent to a contact.
3. Years or decades later, intelligence reports that the document has been found or is circulating.
4. The player must **locate** it (through their network), **suppress** it (through a host with access), and **neutralise** anyone who has already read it.
5. Failure to act means the document becomes part of the institutional knowledge of whoever holds it — permanently raising the awareness level of that faction.
