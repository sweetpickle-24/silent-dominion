# 10.1 Three Levels of Awareness

| **Awareness level** | **Who has it** | **What it means for the player** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cultural legend** | General population, oral tradition, folk belief | Ambient paranoia raised slightly in all regions with exposure history. People attribute unexplained events to the shadow figure. Complicates rumour operations — some rumours get amplified by pre-existing belief. |
| **Institutional knowledge** | Church hierarchies, rival secret societies, some ruling dynasties with long institutional memory | These actors are actively looking for the player's footprint. They do not know where you are but they know what to look for. Any action with elevated exposure risks triggering their investigation. |
| **Personal recognition** | A specific character who has connected enough dots to identify the player's current operational pattern | Crisis state. This character will act on their knowledge — warn rulers, coordinate with rivals, attempt to expose or destroy. Must be managed before they can disseminate what they know. |
