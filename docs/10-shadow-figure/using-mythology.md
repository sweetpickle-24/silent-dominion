# 10.3 Using the Mythology

The player's legend is **not only a threat — it is a tool.** A world that already believes in a powerful shadow figure can be manipulated through that belief. The player can reinforce specific aspects of their mythology to create strategic advantages, without ever confirming their existence.

## Mythology leverage options

- **Protection myth** — allow the belief that the shadow figure protects certain bloodlines to persist. Rulers associated with you gain supernatural legitimacy that makes them harder to depose.
- **Punishment myth** — make examples that reinforce the belief that betraying the shadow figure's interests leads to ruin. Creates a deterrent that costs no ongoing organisation bandwidth to maintain once established.
- **Misdirection** — allow a false version of your mythology to circulate — one that points toward a rival secret society rather than you. Let them be hunted while you operate in the resulting noise.
- **Cult formation** — in certain eras and conditions, allow a religion to form around the shadow figure concept. You are the unseen god. The institution that serves you does your work without knowing you exist.
