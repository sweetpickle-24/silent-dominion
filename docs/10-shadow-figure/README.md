# 10. The Shadow Figure — Player as Legend

Characters in Silent Dominion **know the player exists.** Not as an individual — not by name or face or confirmed identity — but as a concept.

Across centuries of anomalous events, patterns that defy explanation, and advisors whose strategic instincts seem impossibly prescient, the world has developed a cultural understanding that something persistent operates behind the visible surface of history.

They call it different things in different eras. **They are all talking about you.**
