# 14.6 Rival Societies Have the Same Structure

Other secret societies are not individuals — they are organisations with the same layered architecture as the player's. The Architects have their own Lieutenant equivalents, their own Coordinator networks, their own succession problems and geographic coverage gaps.

When the player moves against a rival society they are **not targeting a person — they are targeting an org chart.**

## Attack vectors

| **Attack vector** | **What it does to the rival organisation** |
| --- | --- |
| **Flip a Lieutenant** | Devastating. The rival loses a region's entire coordination layer and gains an intelligence leak at the top. Hardest to execute — requires years of cultivation and perfect execution. |
| **Corrupt a Coordinator** | Useful. Creates an intelligence window into one region. The rival's coverage degrades without them understanding why. |
| **Flood with false intelligence** | Slow but deniable. Degrades the rival's Operative layer by filling it with compromised sources. Their picture drifts from reality. Their automations begin misfiring. |
| **Remove succession candidates** | Long-horizon. Identify the next generation of the rival's Lieutenants and ensure they never reach that position. When the current generation dies, the organisation fragments. |
| **Trigger institutional exposure** | High-risk, high-reward. Engineer circumstances where a ruler or church becomes aware of the rival society's existence and begins a purge. Collateral exposure risk to the player's own network. |
