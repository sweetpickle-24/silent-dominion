# 14.1 The Four-Layer Architecture

| **Layer** | **Who they are** | **What they know** | **What they do** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Player** | The immortal | Everything | Sets strategic direction, touches only Lieutenants, never appears in any record |
| **Lieutenants** | 3–6 trusted individuals, cultivated over years | That the player exists and has been around a very long time. Not who or what the player is. | Coordinate entire geographic regions or functional domains. Filter and route intelligence upward. Manage Coordinators below. |
| **Coordinators** | Regional managers, typically 5–10 per Lieutenant | That they serve a powerful, well-funded organisation. The player does not exist to them. | Gather, consolidate, and route intelligence from Operatives. Manage city-level coverage. Handle local crises independently. |
| **Operatives** | Spies, merchants, scholars, priests doing ground-level work | That they serve someone with money and influence. Nothing above the Coordinator. | Gather specific intelligence, execute specific tasks, cultivate Assets. Expendable. Replaceable. |
| **Assets** | Unknowing contributors — people compromised, bribed, or cultivated without realising it | Nothing. They do not know they are part of anything. | Provide information, access, or capability without active awareness. Lowest risk, lowest reliability. |
