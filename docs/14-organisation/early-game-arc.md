# 14.3 Building the Machine — The Early Game Arc

The game begins with **no organisation at all.** The player is alone in one city with no structure, no network, and no protection beyond their own careful behaviour.

The first generations are the **most personally dangerous in the entire game** — the player has to build the first layer of the machine through direct contact, which means direct exposure.

## Finding the first Lieutenant

Finding the first Lieutenant is the **most consequential decision of the early game.** This person needs to be:

- Capable enough to build a layer beneath them.
- Trustworthy enough to keep secrets across decades.
- Positioned well enough to be useful.

The cultivation process is slow and personal: years of building a relationship, gradually revealing more, testing loyalty through small things before trusting them with larger ones. The wrong first Lieutenant — someone who eventually tries to find out who the player really is, or who cracks under pressure — **sets everything back by decades.**

## The early game machine-building arc

| **Phase** | **What happens** |
| --- | --- |
| **Generation 1–2** | Player operates alone. Direct host contact only. Exposure risk is high but unavoidable. Searching for the first Lieutenant candidate. |
| **Generation 2–3** | First Lieutenant cultivated and trusted. They begin building their Coordinator layer. Player steps back from direct contact with anyone below. |
| **Generation 3–5** | Coordinator layer establishes regional coverage. First Operatives deployed. Intelligence begins flowing without player involvement. |
| **Generation 5–8** | Machine runs reliably in the home region. Player can begin geographic expansion. Second Lieutenant candidate identified. |
| **Generation 8–15** | Multi-region operation. Player manages Lieutenants only. The organisation has its own momentum. |
