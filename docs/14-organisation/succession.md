# 14.4 The Succession Problem

Every person in the organisation **will die.** Plague, war, old age, assassination, exposure — the causes vary but the outcome is constant.

The player's primary organisational management challenge is not building the machine but **keeping it running across the deaths of its key components.**

## Planned vs unplanned succession

A Lieutenant who was properly managed has a designated successor — a Coordinator who has been gradually elevated, who knows enough to step up, who has been introduced to the player through the outgoing Lieutenant before their death. The transition is painful but survivable.

A Lieutenant who dies suddenly, or who was never encouraged to develop a successor, leaves a void that the player must fill through direct contact. **Direct contact means exposure.** The whole chain of the player's safety breaks at the moment they are forced to personally recruit a replacement.

## Succession scenarios

| **Succession scenario** | **Cause** | **Consequence for the player** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Planned Lieutenant succession** | Lieutenant dies with designated successor in place | Minimal disruption. New Lieutenant has 60–80% of predecessor's network intact. Some relationship rebuilding required. |
| **Sudden Lieutenant loss** | Death by plague, assassination, or unexpected arrest with no successor | Player must make direct contact to stabilise the region. Significant exposure spike. Weeks or months of vulnerability. |
| **Lieutenant goes rogue** | Long-serving Lieutenant develops their own agenda, begins acting against player interests | Most dangerous scenario. They know the player exists. Must be managed carefully — removal requires discretion or they expose what they know. |
| **Coordinator chain collapse** | Multiple Coordinators lost in a region due to a crackdown or rival society action | The operative layer in that region goes dark. Intelligence ceases. Rebuilding takes years of in-game time. |
| **Operative attrition** | Normal operational losses over time | Routine. Coordinators replace Operatives without player involvement if the succession infrastructure exists. |
