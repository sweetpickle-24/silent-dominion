# 14.7 The Organisation in the Memoirs System

The [Memoirs automation system](../13-memoirs/README.md) operates **through** the organisation, not around it.

- When a regional dispatch is issued, it routes through the Lieutenant responsible for that region, down to their Coordinators, and executes through the Operative layer.
- The player never touches the execution directly.
- The quality and reliability of the automation is therefore a **function of the organisation's health in that region** — a degraded Coordinator layer produces degraded automation results, regardless of how sophisticated the player's pattern library is.

## Health multiplier

- A region with a **strong Lieutenant, well-maintained Coordinators, and current Operatives** executes automations at full reliability.
- A region where the Coordinator layer was recently rebuilt after a crisis executes automations **slowly and with higher error rates** until the new Coordinators are fully trained.
- A region where the player has been neglecting succession produces **brittle automations** that break unpredictably when key individuals die mid-operation.
- **The organisation's health is the hidden multiplier** on everything the Memoirs system can do.
