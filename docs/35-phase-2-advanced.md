# 35. Advanced Systems (Phase 2)

The following systems are designed for **post-launch or advanced development.** They extend the core mechanics without changing them.

## 35.1 Influence waves

Actions propagate through the world as waves through a social network. An action's effect reaches different nodes at different speeds depending on social proximity, communication infrastructure of the era, and the type of action.

The player can **visualise their past actions as ripples still moving through the world.**

## 35.2 Pattern recognition layer

As the player accumulates centuries of experience, they unlock **pattern recognition events** — moments when the game surfaces a structural parallel to something the player has seen before.

- Recognising the pattern early allows the player to position ahead of it.
- Ignoring it has consequences.

## 35.3 Minority dissent mechanic

**Small actors can overturn large systems.** A single charismatic individual in the right place at the right time can collapse an empire the player spent a century building.

The player must monitor for these individuals and either cultivate them or neutralise them depending on their agenda.

## 35.4 Cultural memory fields

Civilisations develop memory of past events that shapes how they respond to similar conditions.

> A culture that was betrayed by a philosopher-advisor three hundred years ago will be resistant to philosopher-advisors forever.

**The player's past actions leave permanent impressions in the world's cultural memory.**

## 35.5 Inter-society diplomacy

Formal channels between secret societies emerge over time. Societies can:

- Establish **cold peace agreements.**
- Share intelligence on specific targets.
- Trade hosts.
- Declare **shadow war.**

**Shadow war between societies is the most dangerous state in the game** — both sides burn resources aggressively until one is functionally destroyed.
