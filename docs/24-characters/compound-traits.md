# 24.2 Compound Trait Interactions

Traits do **not act in isolation.** Specific combinations produce emergent behaviour that is not predicted by either trait alone.

## The most important compound interactions

- **High ambition + high paranoia** — the most dangerous ruler profile. Expands aggressively, then turns on the allies who enabled the expansion, convinced they want what was built. The player's most productive and most dangerous host type.
- **High intellect + high curiosity** — the character most likely to independently investigate the player's existence. Provides excellent intelligence but is a hunter risk if exposed.
- **High loyalty + high resilience** — the ideal lieutenant profile. Maintains function through crisis, resists rival approaches, unlikely to turn under pressure.
- **High greed + low loyalty + financial access** — the highest corruption probability combination. Should never be placed without oversight.
- **High piety + high charisma** — the religious movement catalyst. Give them the right idea and step back.
- **High paranoia + high ruthlessness + ruler position** — the purge initiator. Does not need much encouragement to eliminate perceived threats, including the player's hosts.
