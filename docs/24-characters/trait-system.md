# 24.1 The Canonical Trait Set

All traits use a **0–100 scale.**

- A score of **50** is neutral — the behaviour you would expect from an average person in this social position.
- Scores below **30** represent a notable deficiency.
- Scores above **70** represent a notable strength or extreme.

### Origins

- **Historical figures** have fixed core scores.
- **Generated characters** have scores drawn from weighted ranges (see [§8.11 Weighted Character Generation](../08-map-and-provinces/weighted-generation.md)).
- **Organisation members** develop their scores through experience and training.

## The ten canonical traits

| **Trait** | **Low (0–30)** | **High (70–100)** | **Primary system interactions** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ambition** | Content with current position; hard to motivate toward greater power | Driven to expand power and status at significant personal risk | Ruler AI aggression; host susceptibility to elevation offers; succession conflict probability |
| **Paranoia** | Trusting, slow to suspect conspiracy, dismisses warning signs | Sees threats everywhere; purges advisors; makes erratic decisions under pressure | Ruler AI threat response; bribery approach selection; manipulation via rumour effectiveness |
| **Loyalty** | Will betray for sufficient incentive; personal interests dominate | Resists external approaches; protects patron even at personal cost | Corruption resistance; bribery susceptibility; response to the player abandoning them |
| **Piety** | Secular; unresponsive to religious framing; sceptical of institutional authority | Deeply devout; responds strongly to religious arguments; may prioritise faith over interest | Religious influence effectiveness; susceptibility to doctrinal manipulation; reformation potential |
| **Intellect** | Simple motivations; predictable; manipulated by blunt approaches | Complex thinker; harder to manipulate crudely; may resist or misinterpret planted ideas | Idea propagation quality; manipulation misfire risk; pattern recognition; counter-intelligence capability |
| **Greed** | Indifferent to wealth; not motivated by financial offers | Highly responsive to financial incentives; will risk a great deal for sufficient payment | Bribery effectiveness; corruption risk; financial manipulation susceptibility |
| **Ruthlessness** | Reluctant to harm others; avoids extreme measures | Will eliminate obstacles without moral hesitation; effective but dangerous as an ally | Action willingness; succession crisis execution; purge probability when paranoid |
| **Curiosity** | Incurious; does not seek new information or ideas | Actively seeks knowledge; susceptible to intellectual seduction; may spread ideas unpromptedly | Idea adoption speed; scholar host value; potential to discover the player's existence independently |
| **Resilience** | Collapses under pressure; trauma changes behaviour radically | Absorbs setbacks; maintains function through crisis; hard to destabilise | Crisis response; manipulation under pressure effectiveness; recovery from exposure events |
| **Charisma** | Low social pull; influences only through formal authority | Natural leader; gathers followers; amplifies ideas they champion | Network building speed; religious movement catalysis; army morale as general |
