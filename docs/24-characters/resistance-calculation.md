# 24.3 Resistance Calculation

When the player attempts to influence a character, **resistance is calculated as a composite of relevant traits** weighted against the action type.

The calculation is **never shown as a number** — the player sees only qualitative signals:

- The host seems receptive.
- The host pushes back.
- The host seems to have someone else's voice in their ear.

## Resistance by action type

- **Idea planting** resists on: intellect (may misinterpret), piety (may reject secular ideas), loyalty to current worldview (existing commitments), resilience (doesn't shift easily).
- **Financial bribery** resists on: greed (inverse — low greed means indifference, not resistance), loyalty, piety (principle), resilience (doesn't bend to incentives).
- **Paranoia amplification** resists on: resilience, intellect (recognises the manipulation), high existing paranoia (already at ceiling), loyalty (trusted relationships buffer against suspicion).
- **Ambition stoking** resists on: resilience, low ambition ceiling, contentment with current status, high loyalty to existing patron.
