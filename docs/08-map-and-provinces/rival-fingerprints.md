# 8.12 Rival Fingerprints — Reading the News

Public news events are not only the world's autonomous output — they are the **visible trace of every faction's activity**, including the player's and all rival societies.

A famine in a prosperous city, a war between kingdoms that were at peace last month, a religious schism in a stable church — any of these might be natural, or they might be the fingerprint of a society that has been operating invisibly in that region.

Learning to read news as rival intelligence is a skill that develops over play and is encoded in the [Memoirs system](../13-memoirs/README.md). It requires accumulating evidence across multiple incidents until a profile emerges.

## The investigation chain

The investigation chain is **tiered** — each level costs organisation bandwidth and time but narrows the picture.

| **Level** | **Data revealed** | **How obtained** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0 — Signal** | Anomalous public event in a region | Automatic. Fires when the simulation generates a public news event that deviates from expected regional conditions. The player may ignore it or flag it. |
| **1 — Mechanism** | The specific cause of the anomaly was artificial, not natural | Basic investigation. An operative determines the famine was caused by grain supply disruption, not drought. A deliberate act is established. |
| **2 — Actor** | A specific individual or institution was involved | Deeper investigation. The disruption traces to a merchant family acting unusually. Their financial connections route through an unrecognised institution. |
| **3 — Pattern** | Multiple events in the region share a common invisible network | Cross-referencing. Three other anomalous events over twenty years share the same financial trail. A persistent organisation is operating here. |
| **4 — Fingerprint** | The organisation's operational signature is identified | Library match. If the player has encountered this society before, the pattern matches a known fingerprint. If not, a new unknown profile is opened. |

## The fingerprint library

The fingerprint library is built through direct experience exactly like the Memoirs manipulation library.

- A player who has never encountered The Pyre has no fingerprint for them — their Level 3 pattern just reads as 'unknown organisation.'
- A player who has fought The Pyre for three centuries recognises their signature at Level 0 and can immediately begin counter-operations rather than spending weeks on investigation.

## Rules

- Each rival society has a distinct operational signature: characteristic event types, preferred methods, typical geographic concentration, financial routing patterns.
- Societies **evolve their methods over time** — a fingerprint built 200 years ago may be partially stale; investigation may update it.
- Rival societies are aware that their fingerprints can be read and actively work to obscure them — varying methods, using cutouts, seeding false trails.
- A Level 4 identification of a rival society in a region unlocks **counter-mandate options** targeting that society's specific network there.
