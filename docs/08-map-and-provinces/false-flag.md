# 8.13 False Flag Operations

The player can engineer public news events that carry the **fingerprint of a rival society** rather than their own. A false flag is one of the highest-cost, highest-impact operations available — it requires deep knowledge of the target society's methods and the operational infrastructure to execute an action that matches their signature precisely.

## Requirements for a false flag

| **Requirement** | **Detail** |
| --- | --- |
| **Fingerprint knowledge** | The player must have reached Level 4 identification of the target society. You cannot convincingly forge a signature you do not know. |
| **Operational capability** | The player must have the actual infrastructure to execute the action. You cannot manufacture a grain supply disruption without a financial network capable of disrupting grain supply. |
| **Stylistic accuracy** | The execution must match the target society's known methods closely enough to fool observers. This is a skill encoded in the Memoirs library. |
| **Clean own-trace removal** | The player's own fingerprint must be absent from the operation. Any trace of the player's network in the event degrades the false flag quality. |

## Quality tiers

| **False flag quality** | **Determined by** | **How it lands** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Poor** | Incomplete fingerprint, rushed execution, own traces present | Identified as inconsistent by anyone with deep knowledge. Creates noise but does not convincingly frame the target. May backfire. |
| **Moderate** | Accurate fingerprint, partial method match, minor own traces | Fools casual observers and lower-level investigators. A thorough investigation may expose inconsistencies. Buys time and confusion. |
| **High quality** | Accurate fingerprint, full method replication, clean trace removal | Convinces most actors including other rival societies. May trigger a false investigation of the framed society. Stands up to scrutiny for months or years. |
| **Perfect** | Complete fingerprint library, precise method replication, zero own traces, active misdirection planted | Indistinguishable from the real thing to all but the framed society themselves. Can permanently damage a rival's reputation with specific factions. |

## Alibi race

The framed society can attempt to disprove the false flag by surfacing their own alibi — demonstrating that their network was not operating in that region at that time. If they can surface this credibly to the relevant actors, the frame collapses and the investigation reorients toward whoever actually did it.

This creates a secondary race: **the player must ensure their false flag is embedded deeply enough that the target cannot easily produce a convincing alibi.**
