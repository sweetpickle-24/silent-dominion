# 8.9 Starting Region — The Mediterranean World

The game begins in **500 BCE in the Eastern Mediterranean.** At start, only this region is fully simulated — Greece, Persia, Carthage, Egypt, and the Italian peninsula.

## How simulation expands

- As decades pass and the player's network extends outward, the simulation expands.
- Getting intelligence from Britain in 400 BCE takes **months** and costs heavily in network resources.
- By the medieval era the world is better connected.
- By the early modern era global reach is feasible.

## Why start here

- The Mediterranean 500 BCE has dense, varied political structures (republics, tyrannies, empires, city-states) that exercise all of the game's political mechanics.
- It has high-density historical figures to anchor the simulation.
- It has the agricultural and trade richness to support the full [resource system](../32-resources/README.md).
- It is the historical origin point of most of the [cycles](../04-world-systems/cycle-engine.md) the player will learn to recognise.
