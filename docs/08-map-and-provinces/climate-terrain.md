# 8.3 Climate and Terrain Effects

Every province has a **climate type** that interacts with its terrain to determine:

- Agricultural cycles.
- Disease risk.
- What resources can be extracted or cultivated.

Climate is not just background — it drives the autonomous world simulation. A cold wet winter in a plains province reduces the harvest. A prolonged drought in a river delta collapses the food supply of an entire region. A warm stable Mediterranean climate allows the agricultural surplus that historically produced classical civilisation.

## Climate zones

| **Climate** | **Region examples** | **Agricultural ceiling** | **Key risks** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Mediterranean** | Greece, Italy, Levant coast | High — olives, grain, wine | Periodic drought; locust swarms |
| **Temperate continental** | Gaul, Germania, Persia plateau | Medium — grain, cattle | Harsh winters; spring flood risk |
| **Arid / semi-arid** | North Africa interior, Arabian peninsula | Low without irrigation | Desertification; water dependency creates strategic vulnerability |
| **Tropical monsoon** | India, Southeast Asia | Very high with irrigation | Monsoon failure collapses harvests; disease incubation high |
| **Northern / boreal** | Scandinavia, northern steppe | Low — short growing season | Extreme winter attrition on armies; population pressure drives migration |

## Gameplay implications

- The player's [weighted character generation](./weighted-generation.md) depends in part on climate stability.
- Climate feeds the [autonomous event system](../09-autonomous-world/natural-events.md) — drought, plague, famine.
- Military campaigns respect seasonal climate (see [§27 Military Forces](../27-military-forces.md)).
