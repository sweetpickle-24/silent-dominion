# 8.5 City Districts — The Innermost Zoom

Every city of sufficient size is divided into **districts** when the player zooms to city level. Districts are not just visual flavour — they are mechanically distinct zones with different economic functions, different host types, different resource outputs, and different strategic implications. A district's character is determined by the city's history, its geography, and what has been built there.

## District types

| **District type** | **Function** | **Host types available** | **Strategic value** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Administrative centre** | Government, courts, tax collection | Rulers, judges, tax officials, court advisors | Political control hub; bribing here affects city-wide governance |
| **Financial quarter** | Banking, moneylending, financial records | Bankers, accountants, financial brokers | Financial network anchor; intelligence on debt and wealth flows through here |
| **Merchant district** | Trade, warehousing, commodity markets | Merchants, traders, guild masters | Trade route intelligence; economic pressure operations originate here |
| **Religious quarter** | Temples, churches, monasteries | Priests, monks, religious scholars, pilgrims | Ideological influence hub; religious movements spread from here |
| **Scholarly district** | Libraries, academies, philosophical schools | Philosophers, scholars, physicians, scribes | Idea propagation; high-intellect host pool; literacy drives |
| **Military quarter** | Barracks, armouries, training grounds | Generals, officers, recruiters | Military intelligence; loyalty manipulation of garrison |
| **Port / harbour** | Docks, shipyards, maritime trade | Sea merchants, ship captains, customs officials | Sea trade routes; smuggling networks; rapid intelligence transit |
| **Common quarter** | Housing, markets, taverns, craft workshops | Minor officials, craftsmen, informants, labourers | Rumour and unrest monitoring; low-cost operative placement |

## Growth

City slots **increase as settlements grow.** Growth is driven by population, which is driven by agricultural surplus, trade income, and stability. A thriving city accumulates population which unlocks new district slots, which allows new buildings, which attracts more trade and population. The player can accelerate this cycle in cities they want to develop or disrupt it in cities they want to weaken.
