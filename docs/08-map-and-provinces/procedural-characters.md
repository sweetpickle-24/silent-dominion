# 8.8 Procedural Characters Anchored to History

Named historical figures anchor the simulation. **Julius Caesar, Cleopatra, Augustus, Charlemagne, Saladin**, and hundreds of others appear at their documented times with their documented roles and personalities.

Around them the world is filled with **procedurally generated individuals** built from era-appropriate cultural and social templates.

## Historical vs generated figures

- **Historical figures** — appear on schedule adjusted by player interference, have fixed core traits, behave consistently with historical record unless the player has acted on them.
- **Generated figures** — built from cultural templates, assigned procedural names, personalities, ambitions from a trait system.
- The player **cannot tell at a glance** which individuals are historical and which are generated. This is intentional.
- Generated figures can become historically significant through the player's influence or world events.
