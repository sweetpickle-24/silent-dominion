# 1. Vision and Core Pitch

*A grand strategy game where you don't rule nations — you shape the forces that make them rise and fall.*

You are an immortal. Not a god, not a monster — just something that has survived too long to be anything other than patient. You have watched empires be born and rot. You have whispered into the ears of kings and watched them march to their deaths thinking the idea was their own. You do not conquer. You persist.

**Silent Dominion** is a sandbox grand strategy game spanning thousands of years of history. The player is a single immortal human — a person with no name the world knows, no face it can pin down, and no death that has ever stuck — who survives and accumulates power by staying close to whoever holds it, then disappearing before they fall.
