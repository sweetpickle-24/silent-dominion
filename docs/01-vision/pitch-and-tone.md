# Vision — Pitch, Uniqueness, Tone

## One-line pitch

> You are history's invisible hand. Every empire that rises, every king that falls, every idea that changes the world — you were there. Nobody knows.

## What makes this unique

- You are a human who cannot die — but you can be found, and finding you is fatal.
- You cannot command armies — you make the general want to march.
- There is no victory screen — only the endless game of staying relevant and staying hidden.
- History is a tool, not a script — events bend around your actions.
- Other secret societies are your real opposition — not nations.

## Tone reference

| **Feels like** | **Does NOT feel like** |
| --- | --- |
| The patience of a spider building its web | Power fantasy with a shadow aesthetic |
| The quiet satisfaction of watching a plan from twenty years ago finally land | Omniscient god game |
| The cold calculation of someone who has seen every type of human before | Linear narrative with a chosen-one arc |
| Never heroic. Never villainous. Just strategic. | Action game with stealth elements |

## Design tone anchors

- **Slow, psychological, creeping, amoral.**
- Never frame player actions in moral terms.
- Restraint and patience are skills. The best players do least.
- The world should feel indifferent to the player. They are one actor among thousands.
