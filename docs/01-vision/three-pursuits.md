# The Three Pursuits

Silent Dominion has no win condition and no end state. Beneath every system is a deeper question: what is the player actually pursuing across centuries? The answer is **three distinct pursuits** that run simultaneously. A player can lean into any one or weave between all three; the game supports all approaches.

| **Pursuit** | **What it is** | **What success looks like** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Machine** | Building durable infrastructure: organisation depth, loyal families, owned entities, persistent institutions, financial networks | An organisation that has survived multiple civilisational upheavals. Lieutenant chains that have run smoothly for ten generations. Banks founded in antiquity still operating in the modern era under different names. The closest the game has to a long-term score. |
| **The Story** | Generating an alternate history through accumulated divergence from the documented timeline | A unique world history that diverges meaningfully from real history. The Caesar who never crossed the Rubicon. The Reformation that came two centuries early. The empire that rose where one historically did not. The chronicle the player has written by playing. |
| **Survival** | Simply continuing to exist across centuries | Every century survived is a victory. A player whose Machine collapsed and whose Story went badly but who is still alive and operating in some reduced form has not lost. Endurance is meaningful in itself. |

## Time horizons

The three pursuits operate at different time horizons:

- **Survival** — immediate, day-to-day. Manage exposure, keep the network running, do not get killed.
- **The Story** — decade-to-century scale. The divergence accumulates as the player's actions ripple outward.
- **The Machine** — generational. The slow construction of something that will outlast every specific era.

## Session-end surfacing

The session-end experience surfaces all three:

- A **chronicle entry** adds to the Story — written in the voice of a future scholar describing what happened in this period.
- A **state-of-the-Machine summary** — current depth of the organisation, thriving families, running entities.
- The simple acknowledgment of **Survival** — another period endured, the immortal still walking the world.

## Playstyles

- Players who lean toward **The Machine** play methodically — building organisation depth, cultivating families across generations, accumulating entities and infrastructure.
- Players who lean toward **The Story** play narratively — pursuing dramatic interventions, watching the world transform under their hand, generating a memorable alternate history.
- Players who lean toward **Survival** play defensively — minimising exposure, accepting smaller ambitions in exchange for resilience, treating endurance itself as the achievement.
- Most great sessions involve all three — the Machine you built lets you pursue Stories you could not otherwise reach, and Survival is what makes both possible.
