# 3.3 What Constrains Player Action

There is no abstract 'influence point' currency in Silent Dominion. The player's ability to act is constrained by **four real, interlocking resources** that together produce the strategic texture of every decision — not a meter draining and refilling, but a complex allocation problem across multiple scarce resources.

| **Constraint** | **What it represents** | **Where it is specified** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Organisation bandwidth** | How many simultaneous operations the lieutenant and coordinator layers can run effectively | [§14 — The Organisation](../14-organisation/README.md). Bandwidth grows with organisational depth and family cultivation. |
| **Financial capacity** | Available funding routed through the financial network with appropriate discretion | [§16 — Financial Networks](../16-financial-networks.md). Capacity is regional, relational, and requires maintenance. |
| **Exposure cost** | How much each action raises the player's detectability across all relevant tracks | [§2.3](../02-player/reputation-and-survival.md) and [§10](../10-shadow-figure/README.md). Exposure decays naturally and can be managed through restraint. |
| **Time** | Real game-time required for operations to execute, intelligence to arrive, and consequences to manifest | [§7.5 Time Controls](../07-interface/time-controls.md) and [§22 Travel Speeds](../22-base-and-travel/travel-speed.md). Most operations take days to years. |

## Example cost breakdowns

Every action the player considers consumes some combination of these four.

- **Bribe of a court official** — financial capacity (the payment), organisation bandwidth (a coordinator handles execution), exposure (variable depending on transaction discretion), time (days to weeks).
- **Cultivating a new host** — bandwidth (an operative's ongoing attention), exposure (cultivation is an action that may be observed), time (often years), little financial capacity.
- **Major strategic moves** — all four heavily.

## How the game scales

This constraint system is what lets the game scale from city-origin to global:

- **Early game** — tight constraints on all four. Thin organisation, modest financial network, low exposure tolerance, no infrastructure for long-horizon plays.
- **Mid game** — more bandwidth and capacity, but operations also scale up in cost.
- **Late game** — enormous organisation with global financial reach, but managing exposure across a vastly larger surface area and dozens of operations running simultaneously.

The constraints feel different at each stage but **they are always the real limit.**
