# 3.1 The Host System

The player's primary interface with the world is attachment to individuals — called **hosts**. A host is any historically significant person the player has cultivated: rulers, generals, philosophers, merchants, priests, spies.

When attached to a host, the player can:

- Subtly bias their thinking toward specific decisions.
- Whisper ambitions, fears, or suspicions into their worldview.
- Access information through their networks and correspondence.
- Unlock deeper influence options unavailable through distant manipulation.

**Hosts are not puppets.** They have personalities, loyalties, and resistance thresholds. A highly ambitious ruler might be easy to push toward war but almost impossible to convince to back down. A philosopher may absorb ideas readily but interpret them in unexpected ways. The player influences — they do not control.

## Host types

| **Host type** | **Influence type** | **Exposure cost** | **Resistance** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ruler / King** | Political decisions, wars, succession | High | Moderate to high |
| **General / Warlord** | Military campaigns, coups, loyalty | Medium | Low (ambition) to high (duty) |
| **Philosopher / Scholar** | Idea propagation, ideology, religion | Very low | High (conviction) |
| **Merchant / Banker** | Economic pressure, trade routes, bribes | Low | Very low |
| **Priest / Prophet** | Religious movements, moral authority | Low | Very high (faith) |
| **Spy / Assassin** | Information, targeted removal | Medium | Low |
