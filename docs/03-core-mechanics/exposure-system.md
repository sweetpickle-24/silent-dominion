# 3.2 The Exposure System

Every action the player takes generates **Exposure** — a measure of how detectable their presence has become. Exposure accumulates across all current hosts, intelligence networks, and rival societies. It decays naturally over time but can spike rapidly with risky moves.

## The five exposure levels

| **Exposure level** | **Range** | **What changes** | **Risk** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Deep shadow** | 0–20 | Full action palette available | None — rivals cannot detect you |
| **Whispered** | 21–45 | Most actions available | Rival societies begin passive surveillance |
| **Known quantity** | 46–65 | High-cost actions restricted | Rulers grow paranoid; may purge advisors |
| **Hunted** | 66–85 | Forced to abandon active hosts | Coordinated action by rivals and rulers |
| **Exposed** | 86–100 | Almost all actions blocked | Multiple factions unite to destroy you |

## Decay and recovery

Recovering from high exposure requires **going dark** — severing active hosts, letting networks lie dormant, and waiting years or decades for the world's attention to move on. This is a deliberate design choice: sometimes the optimal move is to do nothing.
