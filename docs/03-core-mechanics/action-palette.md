# 3.4 The Action Palette

The player's actions are **never direct.** Every action is framed as an influence on a person or system, not a command. Actions are grouped into three tiers by visibility cost.

## Tier 1 — Deep shadow (very low exposure)

- **Plant an idea** — introduce a concept into a host's thinking over months.
- **Seed a rumor** — release controlled misinformation through a proxy network.
- **Observe** — gather intelligence on a person, faction, or situation.
- **Cultivate** — invest in a minor figure to develop them as a future host.
- **Wait** — do nothing. Let the world move. Exposure decays.

## Tier 2 — Active manipulation (medium exposure)

- **Amplify paranoia** — push a host toward suspicion of a specific rival or ally.
- **Stoke ambition** — make a host believe they are destined for more than their current position.
- **Broker contact** — introduce two parties who would not otherwise meet.
- **Disrupt correspondence** — intercept or alter communications between key actors.
- **Advise directly** — provide strategic counsel that shapes a major decision.

## Tier 3 — High intervention (high exposure)

- **Trigger a succession crisis** — accelerate or arrange the circumstances of a ruler's removal.
- **Flip a general** — turn a military leader's loyalty before a decisive campaign.
- **Elevate a doctrine** — push a philosophical or religious idea into mainstream acceptance.
- **Collapse a trade route** — engineer the conditions of economic failure in a region.
- **Betray a patron** — abandon and expose enough of a host's network to cause their downfall.

## Design intent

- There are **no direct buttons.** Every action is mediated by a person or system.
- Tier choice is itself strategy — the same objective can usually be pursued at Tier 1 slowly or Tier 3 quickly.
- Patience is mastery. Tier 1 specialists can achieve everything Tier 3 specialists can, with much lower legend accumulation.
